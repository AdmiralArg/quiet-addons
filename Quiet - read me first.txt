Quiet: small, calm addons for WoW Forever
=========================================

A handful of little addons. Each does one thing, then keeps out of your way.
You don't have to want them all: the game asks which ones you'd like to keep.


Putting them in

  1. Close the game.
  2. Unzip this into your WoW Forever folder, under
         _forever_\Interface\AddOns
     so that you end up with  ...\AddOns\QuietBags,  ...\AddOns\QuietHUD  and so on.
  3. Start the game and log in.


The first time

  A few seconds after you're in the world, a window says hello. It lists the
  addons with a line on each and a switch beside it. They're all on: switch off
  the ones you'd rather not have and press "Apply and reload", or keep them all.

  Changed your mind later? Options > AddOns > Quiet > Choose addons.


Getting new versions

  In your AddOns folder there's now a file called "Update Quiet". With the game
  closed, double-click it: it fetches the newest Quiet addons from GitHub and
  puts them in place. Your settings stay as they are. No account needed.

  Windows may ask "Do you want to run this file?" the first time: choose Run.

  The addons and every version of them: https://github.com/AdmiralArg/quiet-addons


What's in it

  You'll notice these straight away
    Quiet HUD          Fades the interface while you explore. A fight or a target
                       brings it back.
    Quiet Names        Softer names over heads, in a font you like.
    Buff Glow          A buff you let drop pulses on its button until it's back up,
                       and the move to press next gets a soft ring. Every class.
    Zone Levels        Each zone's level range, on the world map.

    These work by themselves: nothing to start. Quiet HUD has one switch at
    the top of its settings that turns all of it off (or type /qhud off).

  There when you ask for them
    Quiet Bags         What your other characters carry: bags, bank and mail.
                       Start: /qbags, or the round button on your bags.
    Quiet Friends      Your friends' full names, one click away when you write
                       a letter. Start: /qfriends to add them.
    Quiet Pins         Notes on the map, and /way.
                       Start: /qpin.
    Quiet Fishing      One button fishes: pole out, cast, reel in, weapon back.
                       Start: put the fishing button on your bar (below), then
                       press it by water.
    Quiet Professions  The cheapest way to level a crafting profession, at
                       today's prices. Start: /qprof (open your profession's
                       window once first).
    Quiet Auctions     Steady gold from the auction house, on the characters
                       you choose. Start: choose its characters in its settings.


Buttons for your bar

  Fishing, Professions, Bags, Pins and Auctions each have a button you can put
  on an action bar (or a controller's crossbar), so you don't need the commands:

    Options > AddOns > Quiet > the addon's name > "Put on my bar"
    (or drag its icon from there onto any bar)


Good to know

  * Settings for all of them are in one place: Options > AddOns > Quiet.
    Each page says how to start that addon.
  * They say little in chat. A new one says hello once, in a small window.
  * To remove one for good, delete its folder from Interface\AddOns.
